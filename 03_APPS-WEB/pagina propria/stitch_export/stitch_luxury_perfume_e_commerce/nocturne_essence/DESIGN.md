## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern luxury ethos rooted in architectural minimalism and nocturnal sophistication. Designed for an ultra-high-end olfactory platform, it serves both discerning individual consumers (B2C) seeking signature scents and trade professionals/boutique stockists (B2B) navigating wholesale volume tiers. 

The aesthetic is austere, deliberate, and sensual. Rather than relying on traditional baroque ornamentation or cliché warm golds, the brand projects authority through obsidian darkness, monospaced technical precision, and luminous platinum typography. Tactile feedback is quiet and confident. Visual noise is eliminated to give bespoke bottle photography, olfactory note pyramids, and volume pricing matrices an art-gallery presence.

Key stylistic pillars:
- **Quiet Brutalism meets Haute Parfumerie:** Sharp, deliberate proportions with generous negative space, precision hairline dividers, and monumental typography.
- **Monochrome & Titanium:** Deep obsidian depths balanced by cool liquid-mercury highlights and icy platinum lettering.
- **Dual-Purpose Utility:** Seamless transition between sensory lifestyle discovery (B2C) and dense, functional procurement tables (B2B) without jarring shifts in brand identity.

## Colors

The palette operates in a tightly calibrated spectrum of cold shadows, mineral tones, and refractive whites. It rejects yellowed golds and saturated hues in favor of pure elemental metals and obsidian basalt.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0C10`):** The primary void. An absolute deep obsidian with subtle cold undertones that creates boundless stage depth.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#12141A`):** Used for primary container cards, flyout drawers, and header states.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1B1E24`):** Used for elevated elements, input backgrounds, active hover states, and nested B2B tier cards.
- **Primary / Platinum Light (`#E5E7EB`):** High-reflectivity platinum white used for primary headlines, critical actions, and maximum visual punch.
- **Secondary / Liquid Titanium (`#94A3B8`):** An anodized cool silver-grey used for secondary metadata, olfactory pyramid notes, and sub-labels.
- **Tertiary / Deep Mineral Slate (`#475569`):** Subdued functional borders, inactive step indicators, and tertiary metadata.
- **Hairline Chrome Accent (`#CBD5E1`):** Applied exclusively at 1px thickness for structural delineation and high-tier callouts.

### Functional Rules
- Maintain an ultra-high contrast ratio of at least 11:1 for all primary editorial text against the `#0B0C10` canvas.
- Avoid gradient fills across large surfaces; limit gradients strictly to high-precision 1px borders simulating edge light on polished glass or steel.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances aristocratic classical literature with industrial technical precision.

### Type Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Editorial Voice (`EB Garamond`):** Used for high-impact display titles, fragrance names, poetic olfactory descriptions, and seasonal collection headers. It brings an intellectual, historical gravitas reminiscent of vintage Parisian scent archives.
- **Interface & Narrative (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, proportioned, and unobtrusive. Renders product details, brand essays, and navigation with effortless clarity and zero legibility friction on retina displays.
- **Specification & Commerce Engine (`Geist`):** Monospaced precision reserved for volume pricing breaks, SKU numbers, batch codes, scent percentage concentrations (e.g., `24% Extrait de Parfum`), and B2B order manifests.

### Typographic Directives
- **Case conventions:** All `label-caps` and `label-mono` must be transformed to uppercase with explicit tracking (`0.12em` to `0.2em`) to maintain sharp, legible luxury proportions.
- **Mobile scaling:** Display headlines automatically drop between 35% and 40% in size on viewports under 768px to completely eliminate awkward line wraps and hyphenations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts an intentional, structured fluid grid system that prioritizes expansive white space (black void) and eliminates any risk of overlapping elements across breakpoints.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max canvas constraint of `1440px` (or `1680px` for immersive lookbooks), with 64px (`margin`) outer margins and 24px (`gutter`) column gaps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with 32px (`margin-tablet`) outer margins and 24px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with 20px (`margin-mobile`) outer margins and 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Content always reflows into single-column vertical stacks.

### Responsive Guardrails
- **No Overlapping Boxes:** Absolutely avoid hardcoded negative margins or absolute coordinate offsets on interactive content blocks. All complex cards (e.g., tiered wholesale pricing tables) flex automatically to 100% width on viewports smaller than 840px.
- **Micro-alignment:** Component interiors follow an 8-point baseline scale using `space-xs` (4px), `space-sm` (8px), `space-md` (16px), `space-lg` (28px), and `space-xl` (48px).

## Elevation & Depth

In a dark minimalist universe, physical drop shadows feel heavy and artificial. The design system creates spatial depth strictly via **tonal stratification** and **refractive edge boundaries**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (`#0B0C10`):** Root background, full-bleed images, and negative space.
2. **Base Containers (`#12141A`):** Low-contrast resting state for cards, lists, and drawers.
3. **Active/Overlay (`#1B1E24`):** Dropdowns, quick-view modals, and hover fills.

### Edge Illumination
- Replace dark blur shadows with crisp 1px borders using `#1B1E24` (subtle) or `#2E3440` (focused).
- When a modal or drawer floats above content, apply a backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`) over an 80% opacity `#0B0C10` scrim.
- Selected or active states employ an ultra-fine titanium hairline: `border: 1px solid rgba(229, 231, 235, 0.4)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs an uncompromising, sharp geometric visual language (`roundedness: 0`). 

Corners are razor-sharp across all UI primitives:
- Form fields, buttons, product tiles, badges, and modal dialogs feature strict right angles (`border-radius: 0px`).
- This angular architectural aesthetic communicates permanence, bespoke craftsmanship, and the crystalline geometry of luxury perfume flacons and lab vials.
- Internal pills or circular shapes are strictly forbidden, with the single exception of round radio toggles for native accessibility compliance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Retail Checkout / Request Sample):** Solid platinum fill (`#E5E7EB`), text in obsidian (`#0B0C10`), font `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600 weight, uppercase with 0.15em tracking. On hover: pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle liquid titanium aura.
- **Secondary Action (Add to Vault / Download Spec):** Transparent background, 1px solid border in `#475569`, text in `#E5E7EB`. On hover: border transitions to `#E5E7EB` and background to `#12141A`.
- **Wholesale Action:** Styled identically to primary but preceded by monospaced tier notation (e.g., `[B2B] INQUIRE TIER 2`).

### Chips & Status Badges
- Zero border radius, 1px border.
- **Fragrance Family Chips (e.g., "WOODY LEATHER", "EXTRACT"):** Background `#12141A`, border `#1B1E24`, font `label-mono`, text `#94A3B8`.
- **Wholesale Status Badges (e.g., "MOQ 12 UNITS", "IN STOCK"):** Border `#475569`, text `#E5E7EB`, monospaced.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#12141A`.
- Border: 1px solid `#1B1E24`.
- Padding: 16px 20px.
- Typography: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, 15px, text `#E5E7EB`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#CBD5E1`. Error states use a high-chroma titanium crimson (`#F43F5E`) with 1px border.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- Background: `#0B0C10` or `#12141A`.
- Structure: Aspect-ratio locked imagery (4:5 vertical ratio) framed with a 1px border `#1B1E24`.
- Content padding: 24px.
- Hover behavior: Subtle image scale (1.02x within an overflow-hidden wrapper) with border shift from `#1B1E24` to `#475569`. No vertical layout displacement.

### Wholesale Tier Pricing Matrix (B2B Component)
- A bespoke responsive data component.
- Visual style: Tabular grid with hairline dividers. Each column showcases Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ), Unit Cost, Margin calculation, and Lead Time.
- Active tier highlight: Outlined in 1px `#E5E7EB` with background `#1B1E24`.
- On mobile devices, the table seamlessly flips to a horizontal swipe card deck or vertical accordion to prevent column compression or text clipping.

### Olfactory Pyramid Accordion
- Three-tier breakdown (Top, Heart, Base notes).
- Features monospaced labels on the left, paired with poetic notes in `EB Garamond` italic on the right, punctuated by a delicate `#1B1E24` dividing line.
